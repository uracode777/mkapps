01 Meal records and photos

The App handles the meal date and time, location category, portion size, estimated meal cost, satisfaction, self-assessed nutrition, notes, and attached photos entered by the user to support recording, editing, history, charts, and statistics. Identifying trends from records and learning notification times are also processed on the device.

Meal records and attached photos are generally stored on the user’s device. The App has no mechanism for collecting meal records or photos on servers managed by the Operator. For iCloud backups, transfers to Apple Watch, and files saved or shared by users themselves, please see the next section.

The meal location is a category selected by the user; the App does not obtain the device’s current GPS location. Nutrition and portion size are recorded as the user’s self-assessment.

02 iCloud, Apple Watch, and backups

iCloud backups

If you enable the Premium iCloud backup feature, meal records and, depending on your settings, attached photos are saved in iCloud storage associated with your Apple Account and used for restoration. The App has no mechanism that allows the Operator to view or retrieve these backups. Apple’s privacy policy and related terms apply to information handled by iCloud.

Apple Watch integration

The App transfers and stores information needed for integration between a paired iPhone and Apple Watch, including meal records, notification settings, and Premium status.

Saving and sharing backup files

At the user’s request, the App can create, save, share, and restore backup files containing records and photos. The terms and privacy policies of the respective services apply at the destinations where files are saved or shared. Backups may include notes and photos, so please check where you save them and with whom you share them.

03 Notifications and device permissions

Depending on the features you use, the App uses the following permissions and system features.

  • Notifications: Local notifications remind you to record meals at times you set or times learned from your meal records.
  • Camera: Photos you take are attached to meal records.
  • Photo selection: Images you choose in the system photo picker are attached to meal records.
  • Adding to the photo library: Attached photos are saved to your photo library when you choose to save them.

For features that request permission, you can change permission settings on your device. If you do not grant permission, the corresponding features may be limited.

04 Advertising and external services

The free version of the App displays ads using Google AdMob and the Google Mobile Ads SDK. It also uses Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) to check advertising consent information and display consent forms, depending on your region and Google’s settings.

According to Google’s official documentation, the advertising SDK may collect and transmit the following information.

  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device- and app-related identifiers, including the advertising identifier when available
  • Information about ad impressions and interactions
  • Information about app launches and interactions
  • Diagnostic and performance information, such as crash information and launch times

This information is used for ad delivery, measuring advertising effectiveness, analytics, and improving SDK quality. The information actually collected varies depending on the SDK version, device settings, consent status, ads served, and other factors. The App has no process for providing your entered meal records, notes, or attached photos to Google for advertising.

For information about advertising data and your choices, please see the following.

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google’s information about advertising
  • Google Mobile Ads SDK data collection disclosure

In regions or settings where reviewing or changing advertising consent is required, an advertising privacy settings option appears on the App’s options screen. You can use it to review or change your consent. You can change your device’s tracking permission in iOS Settings. Changing consent or permissions may affect whether ads are displayed and their content.

In-app ads are hidden while you have Premium.

05 In-app purchases

The App uses Apple’s App Store and StoreKit for Premium purchases and purchase restoration. Purchase and transaction information provided by Apple is used to verify purchase status and provide Premium features.

The Operator does not obtain payment information, such as credit card numbers, through the App. For the handling of payment information and your Apple Account, please see Apple’s privacy policy.

06 Information provided in inquiries

If you contact the Operator, the email address, message, attachments, and other information you send are used to respond to your inquiry, investigate issues, and take necessary action. Please do not send unnecessary personal information or meal records or photos that you do not wish to share.

07 Sharing information with third parties

The Operator does not provide inquiry information to third parties without your consent, except where permitted by law, including disclosures required by law.

For information handled by Apple and Google as part of the App’s features, and for backups you share yourself, please see the relevant sections above. Each provider’s policies apply to the retention, use, and deletion of information sent to its services.

08 Information retention and deletion

You can delete meal records stored on your device from the App’s history screen. Deleting the App also deletes its data on that device, but exported files, photos saved to your photo library, iCloud backups, and data stored on other devices must be managed separately.

Deleting a meal record does not automatically remove it from backup files already created. Delete iCloud backups from the App’s backup screen, and delete exported files and photos in your photo library from their respective storage locations. Check any remaining data on Apple Watch as needed.

The Operator retains inquiry information for as long as necessary to respond or investigate and appropriately deletes information that is no longer needed. To request access to, correction of, or deletion of inquiry information, please contact us using the details below. After verifying your identity, the Operator will respond as required by law and to the extent feasible.

09 Information security

The Operator strives to take necessary measures to prevent unauthorized access, disclosure, loss, and similar incidents involving the information it handles. Please also manage backups you save or share appropriately, including locking your device and checking who has access.

10 Changes to this policy

This policy may be revised in response to changes to the App’s features, external services, laws, or other circumstances. If revised, the latest version and revision date will be posted on this page. Additional notice or confirmation of consent will be provided where required by law.
